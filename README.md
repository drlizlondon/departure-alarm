# Departure Alarm ⏰

> **The gamified, forgiving departure timer built for the ADHD brain.**  
> Live app: **[https://drlizlondon.github.io/departure-alarm/](https://drlizlondon.github.io/departure-alarm/)**

---

## The Idea

Traditional alarms create panic and time blindness. You snooze, get distracted by "one quick thing," and end up rushing out the door stressed.

**Departure Alarm** turns leaving into a game:
* **Pre-departure buzzer**: Alerts you 5 minutes before you need to leave.
* **Ticking countdown bounty**: The early bonus actively ticks down second-by-second on the challenge screen — giving you an instant psychological impulse to grab your stuff and hit **"I'M OUT!"** early.
* **Forgiving at every tier**: Leaving early earns big bonus multipliers (up to 100 coins). Leaving on time earns 40 coins. Up to 5 minutes late still earns 20, and just going always earns 10.
* **Grace buffer buttons**: Feeling overwhelmed putting shoes on? Tap `+2 min buffer 🧘` or `+5 min buffer 🏃` to cleanly extend your window without shame or broken streaks.
* **One-tap checklist**: Quick emoji chips (`🔑 Keys`, `📱 Phone`, `👛 Wallet`, `💊 Meds`, `💧 Water`, `🧥 Coat`) to keep essentials in view.
* **Journey milestones**: Progress through 18 unlocks from *Home* to *Walking shoes*, *Train ticket*, *Boarding pass*, *Private Jet*, and *Moon landing*.
